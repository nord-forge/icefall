# Contributing to Icefall

Thanks for your interest in improving Icefall! Contributions of all kinds are
welcome — bug reports, fixes, features, and docs.

## Sign your commits (DCO)

Contributions are accepted under the [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/).
By adding a `Signed-off-by` line to your commits, you certify that you wrote the
patch or have the right to submit it under the project's [MIT License](./LICENSE).

Sign off automatically with:

    git commit -s

This adds a line like:

    Signed-off-by: Your Name <your@email.com>

The name and email must match your real identity (no anonymous or fake
sign-offs). Commits without a sign-off may be asked to amend before merge.
