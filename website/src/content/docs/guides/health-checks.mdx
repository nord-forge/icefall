Time to complete: about 5 minutes.

A health check tells Icefall whether your app is actually serving traffic. When a
check fails repeatedly, Icefall marks the app unhealthy, can restart it
automatically, and fires notification events.

## Prerequisites

- A deployed app.
- The port your app listens on, and a path that returns a success response for
  HTTP checks.

## Add a health check

<Steps>

1. Open the app, go to **Settings**, and find the **Health checks** card.

2. Choose a check type:

   - **TCP** — Icefall opens a connection to a port. Supply the port (default
     `3000`). A successful connection counts as healthy.
   - **HTTP** — Icefall sends a `GET` request and expects a `2xx` response.
     Supply the port (default `80`) and path (default `/`).
   - **Docker** — Icefall reads the container's built-in `HEALTHCHECK` status, if
     your image defines one.

3. Set the **interval** (how often to check, default 30 seconds) and the
   **failure threshold** (consecutive failures before the app is marked
   unhealthy, default 3).

4. Leave **auto-restart** on if you want Icefall to restart the container when it
   becomes unhealthy. Save.

</Steps>

## Verify

<Steps>

1. The health card shows the current status and an uptime percentage.

2. Recent transitions appear as events: `health.down` when a check starts
   failing, `health.recovered` when it comes back, and `health.auto_restart` when
   Icefall restarts the container.

3. To confirm recovery behavior, temporarily stop the app's listener and watch
   the status change, then restore it.

</Steps>

## Notify on health changes

Health events can trigger notifications. Subscribe `health.down`,
`health.recovered`, and `health.auto_restart` to a channel in
[notification settings](/guides/setup-notifications).

## Troubleshooting

- **Always unhealthy on HTTP**: confirm the path returns `2xx` and that the port
  matches the port your app listens on inside the container.
- **Flapping status**: raise the failure threshold or the interval so transient
  blips do not trip the check.
