Time to complete: about 5 minutes.

Ghost Mode hibernates an app after a period of inactivity, freeing the resources
its containers hold. The app wakes again when you bring it back, which suits
staging environments, demos, and rarely used internal tools.

## Prerequisites

- A deployed app.

## Enable Ghost Mode

<Steps>

1. Open the app and go to the **Settings** tab.

2. Turn on **Ghost Mode**.

3. Set the **idle timeout** in minutes — how long the app sits idle before it
   hibernates (default 30). Save.

</Steps>

When the app hibernates, its status changes to `hibernating` and its containers
are stopped.

## Wake a hibernated app

Bring a hibernated app back from the dashboard's wake control. Icefall starts the
app's stopped containers and returns the status to `active`.

## Verify

<Steps>

1. Leave the app idle past the timeout and confirm its status shows
   `hibernating`.

2. Wake it and confirm the status returns to `active` and the app serves traffic.

</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- **App never hibernates**: confirm Ghost Mode is enabled and that the idle
  timeout has actually elapsed without activity.
- **Wake does not work**: wake only applies to an app in the `hibernating` state;
  an already-active app has nothing to start.
