Time to complete: about 10 minutes.

Google OAuth lets users sign in to Icefall with their Google account. You
configure it once as an administrator by creating OAuth credentials in Google
Cloud and entering the client ID and secret in Icefall.

## Prerequisites

- Administrator access to Icefall.
- A Google Cloud project where you can create OAuth credentials.
- Your Icefall base URL, served over HTTPS.

## Create Google OAuth credentials

<Steps>

1. In the Google Cloud console, open **APIs & Services > Credentials** and
   create an **OAuth client ID** of type **Web application**.

2. Add an **Authorized redirect URI**:

   ```
   https://your-icefall-instance.com/api/v1/auth/oauth/google/callback
   ```

3. Create the client and copy the **Client ID** and **Client secret**.

</Steps>

## Configure Icefall

<Steps>

1. In Icefall, open **Settings > OAuth providers**.

2. In the Google section, paste the **Client ID** and **Client secret**. The
   callback URL is shown there for reference.

3. Turn on the **Enable** toggle and save. The secret field clears after saving
   and shows a configured indicator.

</Steps>

## Verify

<Steps>

1. Sign out, then return to the login page. A **Sign in with Google** option
   should appear.

2. Complete the Google consent screen. You should return to Icefall, signed in.

3. To attach Google to an existing account, open your **Profile** and link the
   Google identity there.

</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

- **redirect_uri_mismatch**: the redirect URI in Google Cloud must match the
  Icefall callback URL exactly.
- **Access blocked**: if your OAuth consent screen is in testing mode, add the
  signing-in user as a test user, or publish the consent screen.
- **Button does not appear**: confirm the Google provider is enabled and saved.
