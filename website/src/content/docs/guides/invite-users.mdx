Time to complete: about 5 minutes.

Icefall supports two kinds of invitations: instance invitations (an
administrator adds someone to the whole instance) and team invitations (a team
owner or admin adds someone to a specific team). Both are sent by email and
expire after 7 days.

## Prerequisites

- For instance invitations: administrator access.
- For team invitations: the owner or admin role on the team.
- SMTP configured so invitation emails can be sent.

## Invite a user to the instance

<Steps>

1. As an administrator, open **Users** and choose **Invite user**.

2. Enter the email address and pick a role:

   - **admin** — full access, including settings and user management.
   - **deployer** — can manage and deploy apps.
   - **viewer** — read-only access.

3. Send the invitation. The user receives an email with a link to set a password
   and join.

</Steps>

## Invite a user to a team

<Steps>

1. Open **Teams**, select the team, and choose **Invite**.

2. Enter the email address and pick a team role:

   - **admin** — invite and manage members.
   - **member** — standard access for deployments.
   - **viewer** — read-only access.

3. Send the invitation. The recipient accepts from the link in their email; the
   email address must match the invitation.

</Steps>

The person who creates a team is its **owner**. The owner role cannot be changed
and is the only role that can delete the team.

## Verify

The team or user list shows pending invitations until they are accepted. Once
accepted, the member appears with their assigned role.

## Troubleshooting

- **No email received**: confirm SMTP is configured and check spam. You can
  reissue the invitation if it expired (invitations last 7 days).
- **"Email does not match"**: team invitations must be accepted by the exact
  address they were sent to.
